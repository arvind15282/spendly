Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and powering filters. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for you.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure database accessible only to your account. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorised access.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces for the interface. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the site. No personal or expense data is shared with Google or any other third party.

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising networks, or any other third-party services that collect user data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app. We will respond within a reasonable time.
